§ 02 How we work

We run it like an experiment

Consulting borrowed most of its habits from sales and almost none from science. We prefer the other order: state what you expect, build the smallest thing that tests it, then check honestly whether it held.

  1. 01
    Observe

    Look first

    We sit with your data, your team and the workflow as it actually runs. A surprising amount of what everyone "knows" turns out never to have been measured.

  2. 02
    Hypothesise

    Commit in advance

    We write down what we expect, and the number that would tell us we were wrong. Agreed before the build starts, so nobody moves the goalposts later.

  3. 03
    Build

    Smallest useful thing

    The least machinery that can genuinely test the hypothesis, put in front of real users. Not a prototype that only works on the demo data.

  4. 04
    Measure

    Report either way

    Against the baseline we agreed on. If it worked, you get the number. If it didn't, you get that number too, plus what we learned and what we'd try next.
